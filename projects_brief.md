## PROJECT 1 — Teal & Amber Contemporary 3BHK

**Images:** WA0017, WA0019, WA0023, WA0037

### Metadata
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Title | The Azure Residence |
| Category | Apartment |
| Client | Private Client |
| Location | Navi Mumbai |
| Duration | 4 months |
| Year | 2024 |
| Area | 1,450 sq ft |
| Budget | ₹28–35 Lakhs |

### Description (Short)
A vibrant yet refined 3BHK transformation centred on a teal, amber, and ivory palette. Every surface — from the herringbone marble-effect accent wall in the dining zone to the sheer floor-to-ceiling drapes — is composed to feel both lived-in and effortlessly elevated.

### Description (Full)
The Azure Residence began as a standard mid-rise 3BHK and was reimagined as a statement of contemporary Indian luxury. The open living-dining plan anchors the apartment's social life, with a teal upholstered dining set beneath an architectural butterfly chandelier creating an immediate focal point. Chevron-patterned wall cladding frames bold abstract artwork, while a brass-framed glass coffee table anchors the seating cluster. Warm amber and teal accent cushions on the charcoal and cream sofas maintain visual harmony across the connected spaces.

The bedrooms continue the palette in a quieter register — vertical slatted panels on the headwall, layered pendant pendants, and botanical-print curtains invite calm and rest. Throughout, cove lighting in the false ceiling adds architectural warmth without a single visible fitting.

### Challenge
Balancing bold, saturated colour accents within an open-plan layout without the space feeling fragmented or visually noisy across three connected zones.

### Solution
A unified flooring material (large-format ivory vitrified tile) was maintained throughout all rooms to visually expand the space. Accent colours were introduced exclusively through soft furnishings and one statement wall per zone, keeping structural surfaces neutral.

### Key Features
- Butterfly gold-leaf chandelier over the dining table as a signature centerpiece
- Herringbone marble-effect wall cladding in the dining zone
- Slatted vertical panel headwall in master bedroom with integrated cove lighting
- Full-height sheer drapery for visual height enhancement
- Teal upholstered dining chairs with contrast amber napery

### Materials Used
- Large-format vitrified tiles (ivory, 800×800 mm)
- Engineered marble-finish wall cladding (chevron lay)
- Fluted MDF vertical panels (slatted headwall)
- Fabric: velvet and cotton-blend upholstery in teal and charcoal
- Brass-finish metal accents (chandelier, coffee table frame)

### Description (Short)
A Scandinavian-inspired 3BHK designed around warmth, simplicity, and biophilic texture. Cream leather seating, a botanical-wallpaper feature wall, and a handcrafted wooden dining set create a home that feels intentionally unhurried.

### Description (Full)
The Birchwood Flat was conceived for a young professional family seeking a home that breathes — uncluttered, warm, and genuinely liveable. The living-dining room is centred on a botanical vine-motif wallpaper in sage and cream, which sets the organic tone for the entire apartment. A Scandinavian-influenced dining set — solid wood table with spindle-back chairs in mixed white and grey — is illuminated by a bespoke cluster pendant chandelier in dark wood and capsule bulbs. The cream leather sofas, paired with a round graphite coffee table, provide a grounded, comfortable anchor for the living area.

### Challenge
Achieving a 'natural' Scandinavian look within the typical compact dimensions of a Thane apartment, without the space feeling staged or cold.

### Solution
All joinery was executed in pale oak-toned laminates; soft furnishing palettes were limited to cream, slate, and natural linen. The botanical wallpaper was applied only to the single dining feature wall, preventing visual overload while defining the zones.

### Key Features
- Botanical vine-motif feature wallpaper in the dining zone
- Spindle-back mixed-tone dining chairs for relaxed elegance
- Bespoke cluster chandelier in dark wood and capsule bulbs
- Full-height sheer drapes in warm grey-taupe
- Round grey concrete-look coffee table as focal point

### Materials Used
- High-quality botanical PVC wallpaper (grey-sage colour)
- Solid wood dining table and chairs (natural teak finish)
- Cream full-grain faux-leather sofa fabric
- Linen-blend curtain fabric (warm grey, blackout lining)
- Frosted glass and dark-wood chandelier components

### Description (Full)
The Terracotta Suite was designed as the crown jewel of a weekend villa retreat in Lonavala. The brief was clear: bring the resort inside. ### Description (Short)
A dramatically confident 3BHK built on a navy-blue, brown leather, and teal-marble palette. Statement lighting and curated art anchor every room, delivering a home that reads as equally powerful and personal.

### Description (Full)
Midnight & Mocha was briefed as a designer home for a high-achieving couple who wanted their apartment to feel like nowhere else. The living room leads with a deep, full-height navy accent wall displaying a pair of abstract geometric artworks, set against which a cognac-brown leather L-sofa and hexagonal cluster chandelier create a lounge of genuine sophistication. The dining area — with its charcoal-leg dining set and fully laid table — flows naturally from the living room, the teal-painted TV unit wall providing easy spatial separation without a structural divide.

Secondary bedrooms follow the same conviction: a deep teal feature wall with gold brass inlay strips carries the TV unit; a blue-grey botanical wallpaper bedroom delivers a quiet garden feel. A separate marble-accent bedroom with a gold sunburst mirror and tufted headboard closes the suite with an art-deco flourish.

### Challenge
Sustaining a bold, dark colour scheme across multiple rooms of a compact apartment without any room feeling smaller or oppressive.

### Solution
Dark accent walls were used on one wall per room only, with remaining walls kept in warm white. Mirrors, gold-tone accents, and high-transparency pendant lighting were used throughout to bounce light and maintain spatial generosity.

### Key Features
- Deep navy blue feature wall with dual abstract artworks in the living room
- Hexagonal-cluster designer ceiling chandelier in walnut and LED

- Botanical-wallpaper secondary bedroom with floating TV shelf and wallpaper panel
- Gold sunburst sunburst mirror on tufted headboard feature wall (art-deco bedroom)

- Full-grain cognac leather sofa fabric
- Teal-tone engineered marble slab (TV feature wall)
- Brass inlay profile strips (6mm, satin gold)

A full-home 3BHK fit-out that pairs warm teak joinery with cream-marble surfaces, a mint-green modular kitchen, and a private mandir — all unified by a distinctive jali-work ceiling detail and a crystal chandelier in the living room.

### Description (Full)
The Ivory Grove is the studio's most comprehensive apartment commission to date — an end-to-end design covering foyer, living-dining, kitchen, three bedrooms, and an integrated home mandir. The foyer sets the tone immediately: a wood-framed entrance pillar with a circular gold backlit mirror and a textured swirl-plaster feature wall creates a moment of arrival before opening into a generously scaled living room with a crystal chandelier and geometric jali ceiling panel above.

Integrating a traditional Vastu-compliant mandir, a contemporary modular kitchen, and hotel-style living spaces within a single apartment without stylistic incoherence.

### Solution
Wood was the material thread: teak-tone joinery appeared across the mandir canopy and shikhara, the kitchen cabinet frames, the foyer pillar, and bedroom wardrobes. This common language allowed each zone to maintain its distinct character while the apartment read as a unified home.

### Metadata

| Title | Prestige WorkSpace — Complete Office Fit-Out |
| Category | Office |
| Client | Corporate Client |
| Location | Belapur CBD, Navi Mumbai |
| Duration | 2.5 months |
| Year | 2025 |
| Area | 2,200 sq ft |
| Budget | ₹45–55 Lakhs |

### Description (Short)
A complete corporate office transformation across reception, boardroom, director's cabin, and collaborative lounge — all unified by a palette of walnut, charcoal grey, and crisp white with bespoke 3D artwork installations.

### Description (Full)
Prestige WorkSpace was a ground-up office interior for a professional services firm seeking an environment that communicates authority, creativity, and global thinking. The reception area — an open-plan lounge with a marble-faced reception counter, pendant dome lights, and a brown leather collaborative sofa — sets a tone of confident professionalism. Glass-partitioned workstations allow natural light to flow freely from the perimeter windows.

The director's cabin pairs a dark chocolate wood executive desk with a 3D map wall installation as a decorative anchor, while the adjacent boardroom — with its yellow-velvet conference chairs and white lacquer table — elevates team meetings to an event. All spaces share the same modular ceiling grid and grey carpet tile base, which keeps costs controlled while allowing each zone to feel customised.

### Challenge
Designing a corporate interior that projects premium quality and brand authority within a leased mid-rise office floor with standard ceiling heights and a fixed structural grid.

### Solution
A consistent material palette (walnut veneer, grey linen, white lacquer) was applied across all rooms in different proportions. 3D decorative wall installations — the white world map in the director's cabin, the woven sculptural panel at the entrance — provided unique artistic identity without requiring custom architectural work.

### Key Features
- 3D modular world map wall installation in director's cabin
- White lacquer boardroom table with yellow-velvet conference chairs for high-energy sessions
- Marble-faced reception counter with industrial dome pendant lighting
- Grey commercial carpet tile flooring across all workspaces
- Full-height glass partitions between zones for light flow and visual connectivity

### Materials Used
- Walnut veneer (cabin joinery, credenza fronts)
- White lacquered MDF (board table, reception counter fascia)
- Carrara marble veneer (reception counter cladding)
- Yellow velvet upholstery (boardroom chairs)
- Commercial carpet tile (grey, loop pile, 500×500 mm modules)
